# P2-R Ouster temporal support

For Ouster, a raw point is eligible for temporal support when its `x`, `y`, and `z` are not NaN and its range is at least `/preprocess/blind` (0.65 m in the pinned TunnelD configuration). This matches the pinned COIN raw-point eligibility rule. The scan end is the ROS message stamp plus the **maximum** `t` offset, in seconds, over all such raw points. Geometry still uses its frozen `filter_rate: 3`; neither the last visited geometry point nor the maximum filtered geometry offset determines the scan end. Other LiDAR types retain their prior timing path.

R0 used the last geometry point visited by `i += g_filter_rate` as the end. On 1179 normal TunnelD frames, the valid-raw maximum exceeded that end by a median 3.900 ms (p95 5.925 ms). On most frames, even the filtered geometry maximum was later than the last visited point; on 29 frames the valid-raw maximum also exceeded the filtered geometry maximum. Synchronization waited until an IMU sample existed after that early end, but propagation consumed only samples at or before it. Thus the propagated history ended a median 5.567 ms before the old scan end. Every normal R0 frame had unsupported COIN acquisition points, with a median 11,335 candidate fallback points and 12,807,677 total.

R1 waits for a real IMU sample at or after the corrected end. When the end lies between two real IMU samples, it linearly interpolates one end sample from acceleration and angular velocity; the right sample remains available to the next window. Interpolation requires an ordered bracket no wider than 50 ms. It does not extrapolate across a gap. The four TunnelD frames with 135.8–192.7 ms brackets are marked `imu_bracket_gap_or_order_invalid`; geometry continues and COIN skips each entire frame.

R2 retains Super's previous propagated states when adjacent Ouster scans overlap in acquisition time. It rebases the retained poses and velocities to the corrected end state with a rigid pose alignment before appending newly propagated states. This is a Super-internal history continuity approximation, not a substitution of official COIN poses. A photo point at `lidar.start_time + offset_time` must have a finite interpolated state in that history; the accepted-frame tolerance is 1 µs at history endpoints. Unsupported or nonfinite acquisition transforms invalidate the whole COIN frame. No accepted frame uses a per-point fallback.

On the final G1 audit, 1180 normal frames have exactly zero valid-raw-max minus scan-end offset; 1176 have complete IMU support and zero unsupported candidate points. Four genuine-gap frames have 22,380 unsupported candidate points in the geometry-only diagnostic. Those candidates are never fused in C1. C1 accepts 1176 photo frames, skips those same four gaps, and records zero fallback points on every accepted frame. `coin_minus_super_scan_end_s` on accepted frames is within about ±0.12 µs, consistent with the raw offset representation.

The R0 and G1 per-frame evidence is in `artifacts/p2r/time_semantics_before.csv` and `time_semantics_after.csv`; their summaries give the exact counts and hashes.
